# Art Direction Resolution

Resolve visual direction before production, but do not turn this into a mandatory client questionnaire.

## Output: Art Direction Brief
### Visual Intent
What should the audience perceive/feel?

### Visual Thesis
One concise design proposition that explains why the system should look this way. Avoid empty adjective stacking.

Example:
> Quiet Precision — express technology through controlled spacing, restrained material, and precise reflection rather than neon HUD clichés.

### Reference Translation
For each reference:
- role,
- what to take,
- what to preserve,
- what not to copy.

Reference roles:
- PRODUCT_REFERENCE
- INSPIRATION_REFERENCE
- EXECUTION_REFERENCE
- SCENE_REFERENCE
- NEGATIVE_REFERENCE
- PROOF_REFERENCE
- BRAND_REFERENCE

### Negative Direction
Define both:
- **Anti-board:** visual directions not wanted.
- **Anti-slop:** generic AI filler/clichés to avoid.

Examples:
random HUD, meaningless micro-text, generic luxury black-gold, purposeless neon glow, irrelevant glass cards, decorative data with no meaning.

## Rule
Art Direction is a resolution process. Persist only the approved outputs into the Campaign Visual System, not the full hidden reasoning.
