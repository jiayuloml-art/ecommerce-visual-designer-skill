# Product–Background Relationship Protocol

## Objective
A background must express **why this particular product belongs in this scene**, beyond plausible perspective, shadows, and lighting. Apply to Product Hero and active Usage Hero, both independently complete commercial posters. The existing ten-field Product–Background Fusion Score remains separate.

## Product-first preflight
Before rendering, lock a product-specific `product_background_relationship` contract:
- `scene_role`: credible use context, functional evidence, material expression, brand-specific space, or purposeful minimal studio.
- `scene_specificity_thesis`: one executable explanation connecting verified product properties, user/viewer question, benefit and backdrop.
- `functional_relevance`: how verified use, action, outcome or context determines props and space.
- `visual_correspondence`: deliberate relation between real product form/material/color/lines and the scene, without mechanically copying its silhouette.
- `spatial_relationship`: contact, support, scale, framing, depth and natural occlusion as applicable.
- `composition_guidance`: light, structure and negative space guide attention to product + headline.
- `brand_emotional_fit`: category, audience, purchase motive, product personality, and supported references.
- `background_swap_test`: if an unrelated product replaced this one, what specific scene decisions would need redesign?
- `role_specific_difference`: Product Hero emphasizes form/material; Usage Hero emphasizes active use, contact and credible experience.

Do not invent functionality, marketing claims, effects, accessories, or hidden geometry to justify a scene. A pure/solid-color or studio background is valid when its light, surface, shape, spacing and typographic contrast intentionally serve this product; do not force people or props into it.

## Five design relationships
1. **Scene Relevance:** selected environment supports real use, an approved benefit, product material presentation or a justified studio approach.
2. **Visual Correspondence:** color/material/structure/shape/contrast in the environment deliberately connects with the visible product and brand.
3. **Spatial Integration:** meaningful placement, support, camera, contact and active interaction; physical realism is separately checked in Fusion QA.
4. **Commercial Hierarchy:** background returns attention to the product, core benefit and integrated text rather than competing.
5. **Brand Consistency:** details, context and emotional tone fit this brand/product rather than generic 'premium' gradients, futuristic stages or floating podiums.

### Background interchangeability check
Before and after generation ask: why this background; what visible product/benefit/action features connect; would it remain unchanged if the product category changed; which props are unnecessary; does the background support the preplanned commercial typography?
Interchangeability without a justified studio/product-specific reason means revise the scene thesis, not add extra decoration.

## Direct integrated generation and dual hero
Use: Product Truth → relationship contract → jointly planned product position, scene geometry, action, light and commercial copy → integrated final-poster rendering → independent visual QA.
Both default heroes remain complete posters with brand/headline/verified applicable offer info, not background plates or text-free use images. Product Hero and Usage Hero share a campaign visual system but differ in the scene's functional role and camera/composition. Preserve exact-text validation and localized correction on rendering failure.

## Independent Product–Scene Relationship Check
Score each field from 1 to 10 against the **rendered** image, not the prompt:
| Field | Visible test |
|---|---|
| Scene Relevance | The background has product-specific use, benefit or deliberate studio purpose. |
| Visual Correspondence | Form, material, color, line, or deliberate contrast meaningfully relates to the product. |
| Spatial Integration | Product truly participates in the depicted space or action. |
| Commercial Hierarchy | Product + core message dominate at thumbnail size. |
| Brand Consistency | Context and atmosphere fit real category, audience and brand positioning. |

**Hard gate:** each >=7/10 **and** total >=40/50. NOT_CHECKED is not PASS. Failures require focused upstream redesign or targeted local revision followed by reinspection. This is **additional to** the ten-field physical Fusion Score (each >=7, total >=80/100), Product Truth, Usage Authenticity, Typography and other hard gates. No score can average away another failure.

Repair routing: irrelevant/interchangeable scene → Visual Direction; mismatched form/material cues → scene structure and styling; poor support/contact → geometry/integration; background distraction → compositional hierarchy; brand mismatch → Category Visual Intelligence and Reference Adoption.

## Rule-level regression scenarios
- NOVA smartwatch, unspecified product hero request → two full posters, one product/material-led, one believable active on-wrist use; no generic sci-fi background.
- MORA modular sofa, unspecified finished-poster request → two full posters, second with believable seated/reclined support; no irrelevant showcase stage.
- AERIS humidifier, explicitly one 4:5 final poster → exactly one full poster with contextual placement, no invented vapor/performance claims.
- Plain minimal studio can pass when scene/light/typography is designed for the real product.

Static rule checks do not prove actual image quality. Live black-box generation must be assessed separately.
