# Platform Adapters

Platform adapters modify a medium/job combination; they do not replace medium grammar.

## Adapter schema
- Platform
- Surface
- Typical entry state / intent level
- Native content expectation
- Information bias
- Interaction pattern
- Commerce role: discovery / consideration / comparison / transaction / retention
- Trust architecture
- Valid action path
- Content constraints
- Input implications
- Platform-fit QA

## Trust architecture
- Product Truth
- Content Trust
- Transaction Trust
- Platform Assurance

## Current archetype notes
### Taobao / Tmall
Commerce-first / content-enhanced. Product discovery, evaluation, detail, video/live, and transaction can coexist. Category rules matter.

### JD
Commerce-first with strong product-information and decision-support requirements; product images, videos, detail, search/recommendation surfaces may carry different local jobs.

### Xiaohongshu
Content-first / commerce-connected. Relevance, experience, authenticity, and platform-valid action paths matter. Avoid fake consumer voice or unverified testimonial framing.

### Douyin
Content-commerce multi-surface. Short video, live, search, mall, product card, and image/text surfaces can play different roles. Do not equate platform with short video.

### Xianyu
C2C condition-and-trust commerce. Current item condition, real photos, defects, accessories, seller/transaction trust, and transparent proof are often more important than advertising polish.

### Dewu
Authenticated/curated commerce + lifestyle community. Platform assurance and product identity can be central trust sources.

## Rule
Always resolve `Platform + Surface`; platform name alone is insufficient for production.
