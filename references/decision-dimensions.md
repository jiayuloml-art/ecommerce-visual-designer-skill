# Decision Dimensions

Use these internally to diagnose what materially changes strategy, inputs, or outputs. Do not expose taxonomy labels in CLIENT MODE.

## Role taxonomy
- **D — Decision Dimension:** changes strategy/input/output systematically.
- **S — Strategy Variable:** inferred/recommended design decision.
- **C — Constraint:** limits what is possible.
- **I — Input:** fact, asset, data, reference, or material needed.
- **O — Output:** deliverable or artifact.
- **M — Interaction Mechanism:** approval, decision state, Human Gate.

## Dimension tests
- **T0 Role test:** “How should design differ?” = likely D. “What do current resources allow?” = likely C.
- **T1 Operationalizable:** can it become a stable choice/category?
- **T2 Input effect:** does it change required input?
- **T3 Output effect:** does it materially change output?
- **T4 Independence:** is it meaningfully distinct from existing dimensions?

## D1 Product
### Primary design class
- Wearable / fit-oriented
- Use-effect-oriented
- Sensory consumption
- Functional / technical
- Spatial
- Complex durable goods
- Simple utility

### Secondary tags
Wearable, Technical, Sensory, Spatial, Process-based, Evidence-heavy, Style-driven, Variant-heavy, High-involvement.

### Selling structure
Single SKU, variants, multi-spec, series, set, bundle, configurable.

### Product condition / circulation
New, second-hand, refurbished, display/sample, custom/handmade.

## D2 Market & brand positioning
### Market tier
Value, Mass, Upper-mid, Premium, Luxury/Collectible.

### Core value positioning
Choose 1 primary and at most 2 secondary when useful:
price-performance, professional performance, technology, quality/craft, design/aesthetics, health/natural, trend/self-expression, lifestyle/emotional, sustainability/responsibility, heritage/scarcity.

Visual adjectives such as minimal, futuristic, youthful are strategy outputs, not D2 facts.

## D3 Consumption / use scenario
Prefer one primary scenario plus up to two secondary scenarios.
Represent internally as:
**Environment + Activity + User Role + Situation Intensity**.
Include use scenarios and occasion scenarios such as gifting/holiday/travel.

## D4 Platform / channel
Resolve:
- platform archetype,
- concrete platform,
- surface,
- decision state: Fixed / Candidate / Open.

If Candidate/Open, recommend before asking the client to decide.

## D5 Commercial context
### Product commercial stage
Pre-launch, Launch, Growth, Regular sales, Upgrade/Replacement, Late/Clearance.

### Campaign context
Regular, Promotion, Seasonal/Holiday, Launch event, Collaboration/IP, Social/topic event.

Treat as a contextual modifier unless it materially changes output.

## D6 Communication barrier
Choose 1 primary and at most 1 secondary:
- P1 Low awareness
- P2 Weak attraction
- P3 Poor understanding
- P4 Insufficient perceived value
- P5 Lack of trust
- P6 Decision/conversion friction
- P7 Insufficient use education
- P8 Brand perception mismatch

Evidence state: Known / Suspected / Unknown.

## Diagnostic rule
Do not force every project to fill every dimension. Resolve only the dimensions that materially change the project.
