# Strategy and Communication Jobs

## Strategy outputs
- **S1 Core Communication Focus** — what is most important to communicate now?
- **S2 Message Hierarchy** — Primary → Secondary → Supporting.
- **S3 Proof / Trust Strategy** — what evidence or trust source is required?
- **S4 Platform Strategy** — what role should each platform/surface play?
- **S5 Visual Direction** — the initial perceptual direction before art-direction resolution.
- **S6 Campaign Scale** — Compact / Standard / Extended when useful.

Prefer one primary strategy route. Offer alternatives only when trade-offs are meaningful.

## Communication jobs
Jobs describe the user-state change an output should create.

### J1 ATTRACT
Seen → willing to continue.
Typical mechanisms: hook, focal contrast, novelty, concise headline.

### J2 ESTABLISH_RELEVANCE
Seen → “this is for me / my situation.”
Typical mechanisms: scenario, pain/desire, identity, use context.

### J3 EXPLAIN
Unclear → understood.
Typical mechanisms: feature→benefit translation, diagram, flow, demonstration.

### J4 BUILD_VALUE
Understood → worth considering / worth the cost.
Typical mechanisms: benefit, comparison, long-term value, experience upgrade.

### J5 BUILD_TRUST
Doubt → credible.
Typical mechanisms: proof, demo, data, authentic use, testimonial, certification when verified.

### J6 REDUCE_DECISION_FRICTION
Interested but hesitant/confused → easy to choose.
Typical mechanisms: SKU guide, comparison, FAQ, compatibility, service, warranty, size guidance.

### J7 DRIVE_ACTION
Intent → concrete action.
Subtypes:
- CLICK_THROUGH
- PURCHASE
- ENGAGE / ENQUIRE

Always resolve desired action and platform-valid destination.

### J8 EDUCATE
Does not know how to choose/use → can choose/use correctly.
Typical mechanisms: tutorial, usage flow, setup, care, replacement guidance.

## Communication mechanisms
Mechanisms are methods, not top-level jobs:
HOOK, PAIN/DESIRE, SCENARIO, FEATURE, BENEFIT, DEMONSTRATION, PROOF, COMPARISON, SOCIAL PROOF, DATA, TESTIMONIAL, BEFORE/AFTER, FAQ, PRICE/OFFER, CTA.

## Job assignment
- Campaign may have one dominant communication problem.
- Each output should have one primary job and, when useful, at most one secondary job.
- Different pages/shots within one sequence may have different local jobs.
